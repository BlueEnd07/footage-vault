Play YouTube-only videos like your others

  1. Open takeout.google.com with your channel's Google account and click Deselect all.
  2. Tick only YouTube and YouTube Music. Under All YouTube data included, keep just videos and video metadata.
  3. Next step → Create export. Google emails you when it's ready (minutes to hours). Download and unzip it.
  4. Click below and paste the unzipped folder's path (the one containing Takeout).

Nothing is uploaded again. After the usual grace period, the Takeout files go to the Recycle Bin like other originals, so keep the zip if you want the originals.
